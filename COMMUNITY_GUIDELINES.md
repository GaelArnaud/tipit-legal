# Community Guidelines
_Last updated: 6 August 2026_

tipit works because a tip means something. These rules keep it that way. They apply to everything you post — tips, photos, comments, lists, profiles — and they are part of our Terms of Use.


## Be genuine

Recommend places you actually know and like. No fake reviews, no undisclosed paid promotion, no posting on behalf of a business pretending to be a friend. If you own or work for a place, say so.


## Be respectful

No hate speech, harassment, threats, or content that demeans people for who they are. Disagree about restaurants all you like; attack the paella, not the person.


## Respect privacy

Don't post other people's personal data — home addresses, phone numbers, precise locations of private homes — without their consent. Don't post photos of identifiable people who wouldn't want to appear. Use the approximate-location option when a place deserves discretion.


## Keep it legal and safe

No sexually explicit content, no glorification of violence, no promotion of illegal goods or activities, no content endangering minors — ever, in any form.


## No spam

No mass posting, engagement bait, misleading tags, or manipulation of likes and follows.


## Reporting and enforcement

Every tip and profile can be reported in the app. We review reports promptly, remove content that breaks these rules, and warn, restrict or ban accounts depending on severity and repetition. Illegal content is removed and may be reported to authorities. To appeal a decision or report something directly, write to contact@thetipitapp.com.
