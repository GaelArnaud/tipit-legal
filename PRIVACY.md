# Privacy Policy
_Last updated: 6 August 2026_


## 1. Who we are

tipit is operated by TheTipitApp, France ("we", "us"). We are the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. For anything privacy-related, write to contact@thetipitapp.com.


## 2. What data we collect

- Account data: email address, username, display name, and optionally an avatar, bio and profile location.
- Content: the tips you post — text, photos, tags, the place they refer to — plus your lists, likes, comments and follows.
- Location data: the coordinates of the places you tip. If you enable it, your device location is used to show nearby tips and to attach a place to a new tip. You can post with an approximate location (deliberately offset) and set a home area that triggers a privacy warning before you post too close to it.
- Technical data: device type, app version, language, and a push notification token if you enable notifications.
- Waitlist: if you sign up on our website, we store your email address and language.


## 3. Why we process it

- To provide the service (contract): accounts, posting, maps, feeds, sharing with the audiences you choose.
- With your consent: device location, push notifications, and any optional features that ask first. You can withdraw consent in your device or app settings at any time.
- Legitimate interest: keeping the service secure, preventing abuse, and moderating reported content.


## 4. AI features

The Memory Jogger feature analyses photos you choose to submit in order to suggest a draft tip (for example, a short label for what the photo shows). Photos submitted to this feature are processed by our AI provider, Anthropic, acting as a processor, and are not used to train their models. You choose which photos to submit; nothing is analysed without your action.


## 5. Who we share data with

We do not sell your data and we do not run advertising. We share data only with processors that run the service for us: Supabase (database, storage and authentication, hosted in the EU — West Europe region), Cloudflare (website hosting and waitlist page delivery), Anthropic (photo analysis for the Memory Jogger feature only), and Apple (push notifications and app distribution). Each processes data under contract and only on our instructions. Other users see your content strictly according to the visibility you set on each tip.


## 6. Where your data lives

Our primary infrastructure is hosted in the European Union. Where a processor transfers data outside the EU, the transfer is covered by an adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses.


## 7. How long we keep it

Your data is kept while your account is active. When you delete your account (available in the app under Settings), your profile, tips, photos and files are deleted; we retain only a minimal, irreversibly hashed record of the deletion itself for audit and abuse-prevention purposes. Waitlist emails are deleted once launch communication is complete or on request.


## 8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can access, correct, delete, and receive a copy of your data, object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent. Write to contact@thetipitapp.com and we will respond within one month. You can also lodge a complaint with the CNIL (cnil.fr) or your local supervisory authority.


## 9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, access is controlled by row-level security rules in our database, and secrets are stored using your device's secure storage. No system is perfectly secure, but we design with privacy as a feature, not an afterthought.


## 10. Minimum age

tipit is not intended for children under 15. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone below that age; if you believe a child is using the service, contact us.


## 11. The website

thetipitapp.com sets no cookies and runs no analytics or trackers. The only data the site collects is the email you voluntarily submit to the waitlist.


## 12. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the app.
