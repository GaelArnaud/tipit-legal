This policy explains what personal data tipit collects, why, and what your rights are. It applies to the tipit mobile application and related services. We process personal data in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and French law.

1. Data we collect

Account data. Email address, username, display name, password (stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider), optional bio, avatar photo, declared location, interests, and language preference.

Content you post. Tips (text, photos, videos, links, labels/tags), lists, comments and replies, likes, saves, retips, and messages, together with the visibility level you choose for each tip.

Location data. Coordinates you attach to tips — either from a photo's metadata, your device's GPS (with your permission), or a point you choose on the map. If you enable approximate location, we deliberately offset the stored coordinates by 100–600 metres at the time of saving; the precise point is not kept. You may save an approximate home area so the app can warn you before posting a public tip near it. Your declared profile location may be verified for the "Local" feature.

Photos and videos. Media you attach to tips or set as your avatar, stored on our hosting infrastructure. Camera and photo library access happen only with your permission.

Memory-Jogger. If you use this feature, the photo you select is sent to an AI model (operated by Anthropic) to suggest a short draft label and description. The photo is used to generate the suggestion and is not used by us for any other purpose.

Push notifications. If you enable notifications, we store a device push token, and your notification preferences.

Usage data. We record in-app events (such as app opens, tips posted, tips viewed or displayed, likes, saves, searches, follows) linked to your account, to understand how the Service is used and to show creators how their tips perform. We do not use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

Reports and moderation data. Reports you submit or that concern your content, and blocks you create.

3. Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers. Data is processed by service providers acting on our instructions:

Supabase — database, authentication, file storage, and serverless functions; our project is hosted in the European Union. Apple — app distribution and, where you enable them, system services. Expo — build and update infrastructure and push notification delivery. OpenStreetMap / Nominatim — converting coordinates to place names and searching places; search text and coordinates are sent to the geocoding service. Anthropic — AI processing for the Memory-Jogger feature only.

Other users see what your visibility settings allow. We may disclose data where required by law, or to protect users or third parties from serious harm.

4. Retention

Your data is kept while your account exists. When you delete your account: your profile, tips, media, and messages are deleted; your usage events are irreversibly anonymised (unlinked from any identity) so that aggregate statistics remain accurate; and a minimal deletion record (a hashed email and basic counters, from which your address cannot be recovered) is kept for security and abuse prevention. Encrypted backups roll off within 90 days. We may retain data longer where the law requires it or to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

5. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, and erase your data, to restrict or object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time (without affecting prior processing). Most of these are available directly in the app — editing your profile, changing visibility, deleting content, deleting your account. For anything else, contact [SUPPORT EMAIL]; we respond within one month.

You may lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority; in France, the CNIL (cnil.fr).

6. Children

tipit is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly process their data. If you believe a child under 16 uses the Service, contact us and we will delete the account.

7. Security

Data in transit is encrypted (TLS). Access to content is controlled by row-level security rules enforced in the database. Credentials on your device are stored in the iOS secure enclave (Keychain). No system is perfectly secure; notify us immediately of any suspected breach of your account.

8. International transfers

Our primary infrastructure is in the European Union. Where a provider processes data outside the EU (for example, AI processing or push delivery), transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's adequacy decisions or Standard Contractual Clauses.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. For material changes, we will notify you in the app or by email before they take effect.
